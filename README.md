# slidedeck is now carouseldeck

This repository is archived. slidedeck was renamed **carouseldeck** and moved into [pedrosousa13/deck-cool](https://github.com/pedrosousa13/deck-cool), under [`decks/carouseldeck`](https://github.com/pedrosousa13/deck-cool/tree/main/decks/carouseldeck), with its full history.

- **Packages:** [`@carouseldeck/react`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@carouseldeck/react), [`@carouseldeck/react-18`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@carouseldeck/react-18), [`@carouseldeck/core`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@carouseldeck/core) and [`@carouseldeck/docs`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@carouseldeck/docs). Every `@slidedeck/*` version is deprecated.
- **Site and docs:** [carousel.deck.cool](https://carousel.deck.cool). slide.deck.cool redirects there.
- **Issues:** [pedrosousa13/deck-cool/issues](https://github.com/pedrosousa13/deck-cool/issues). The open issues from here were moved there.

To upgrade, change `@slidedeck/*` to `@carouseldeck/*` in your imports, and rename `data-slidedeck-*` to `data-carouseldeck-*` in your CSS. The [1.2.0 changelog](https://github.com/pedrosousa13/deck-cool/blob/main/decks/carouseldeck/packages/react/CHANGELOG.md) has the details.
